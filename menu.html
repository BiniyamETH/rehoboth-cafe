Ethiopian Food & Coffee Menu

Ethiopian breakfast favourites, hearty lunch and dinner plates, and cold drinks at Rehoboth Cafe, 2737 Danforth Ave, Toronto.

Breakfast

  • Tibs Firfir

    $17.99

    A delightful combination of tender tibs meat and firfir, creating a savoury and satisfying meal.

  • Ful

    $14.99

    A savoury and filling dish made from fava beans, seasoned with spices, and often garnished with fresh vegetables and egg (boiled or scrambled) and some kibe, olive oil or sesame oil.

  • Chechebsa

    $15.99

    Small, bite-sized pieces of flatbread sautéed with spices and butter or oil. Made with whole wheat or white wheat, prepared with olive oil or kibe (clarified butter).

  • Firfir

    $15.99

    A spicy and tangy dish made from shredded injera (Ethiopian flatbread) mixed with a rich berbere sauce.

  • Genfo

    $17.99

    A hearty and nutritious porridge for a comforting start to your day. Made with whole wheat or white wheat, prepared with olive oil or kibe (clarified butter).

  • Bula

    $16.99

    A traditional Ethiopian breakfast made from the starch of the enset plant, with a unique and wholesome flavour.

  • Waffle

    $9.99

    Crisp, golden waffles with your choice of toppings: mango, banana or blueberry.

Lunch & Dinner

  • Shiro

    $16.99

    A rich and creamy chickpea stew, slow-cooked with a blend of spices. Often enjoyed with injera.

    • Vegetarian

  • Tibs

    $23.49

    A traditional Ethiopian stir-fry of tender beef, lamb or goat, sautéed with onions, peppers and an aromatic blend of Ethiopian spices. Served with injera.

  • Misir Wot / Adess

    $16.99

    A hearty lentil stew slow-cooked with a blend of spices and herbs. Served with injera.

    • Vegetarian

  • Kitfo

    $24.99

    Finely minced raw beef seasoned with mitmita (a spicy chili powder) and niter kibbeh (clarified butter). Served with injera or kocho.

  • Special Kitfo

    $27.99

    Kitfo mixed with ayib (cottage cheese) and gomen (kale).

  • Bula Kitfo

    $27.99

    An enhanced version of the classic kitfo, served with bula for an extra layer of flavour and texture.

  • Rehoboth Special (Meal for 3)

    $59.99

    A meal for three, including tibs, firfir and kitfo. A delightful and diverse tasting experience for sharing.

    • For sharing
