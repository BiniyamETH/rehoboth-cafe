About Our Ethiopian & Eritrean Cafe

Your neighbourhood cafe on Danforth Avenue, serving Ethiopian and Eritrean favourites and freshly brewed coffee.

Our story

Good food brings people together

At Rehoboth Cafe we serve authentic Ethiopian and Eritrean favourites and freshly brewed coffee in a warm, welcoming space on Danforth Avenue, Toronto.

Stop in for breakfast, sit down for lunch or dinner, or pick something up to take home.

What you'll find

Breakfast, lunch, dinner and coffee

  • Ethiopian & Eritrean breakfast

    Ful, Firfir, Tibs Firfir, Chechebsa, Genfo, Bula and waffles.

  • Lunch & dinner

    Tibs, Kitfo, Special Kitfo, Shiro, Misir Wot and the Rehoboth Special for three.

  • Coffee & drinks

    Freshly brewed coffee, plus cold drinks to go with your meal.

A cup with a long history

Ethiopia is widely known as the birthplace of coffee, and sharing coffee is a cherished part of Ethiopian and Eritrean hospitality. That spirit of slowing down and sitting together is what we hope you find at our table.
